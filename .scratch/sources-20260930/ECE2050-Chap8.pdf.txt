One-Shot LogicsqWhen a pulse is applied, the output of gate G1 goes LOW. This HIGH-to-LOW transition is coupled through the capacitor to the input of inverter G2. This G2 HIGH output is connected back into G1, keeping its output LOW. qThe capacitor immediately begins to charge through R toward the high voltage level. The rate at which it charges is determined by the RC time constant. When the capacitor charges to a certain level, which appears as a HIGH to G2, the output goes back LOW.
A single narrow trigger pulse produces a single output pulse whose time duration is controlled by the RC time constant.
