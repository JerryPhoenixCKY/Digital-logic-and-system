## Corrections and source traps to carry into the explanations

1. **CRC:** generator1010 has degree3. Data10110010 must append3zeros; independently computed remainder110 gives transmitted10110010110. The12-bit printed101100100110 in2020Finalp2/2021Midtermp3 is inconsistent with that specification. For2025Midtermp3, data1101/generator101 yields110110 (remainder10).
2. **Numerical typo:**2025Midtermp2's printed prose13−(−10)=23 does not match the question00001100−11110110. Correct arithmetic is12−(−10)=22, result00010110.
3. **Floating format:**2025Midtermp10's custom16format1+4+11,bias7 must be labeledcustom. IEEEbinary16 is1+5+10,bias15. Normal-number decoding excludes exponentallzeros/allones cases; do not overgeneralize the slideformula.
4. **Pulse frequency:** a modulus20000 terminal-count event from100MHz occurs at5kHz but has a short dutycycle. Calling it aclock in the course diagram does not establish a50%duty squarewave.
5. **Clock/control semantics:** positive-edge and negative-edge textbook illustrations can occur on one page; actual question wording and actual symbols prevail. 74HC163's clear/load are synchronous; 74HC195's clear is asynchronous. Part-specific semantics must be stated before a waveformtrace.
6. **Arithmetic paths:** CLA propagation may useORorXOR; either is valid with consistentcarryequations, but sum generation still usesXOR. A multiplier timinganswer depends on its actualaddertopology and cannot become a universal8.1tformula.
7. **T/F keys are fallible:**2021Finalp1 Q1(6) marks a low-frequency ripplelimitationasY. Cumulativedelay limits maximumusablefrequency; the notes teach the reasoning instead of memorizing thatkey.
8. **Active-level interpretation:** active-lowdecoder outputs require NANDcombiningONminterms, not plainOR; encoderzero needsvalidity ifnoinput must bedistinguished; HIGH/LOW and asserted/deasserted are separate meanings.
9. **Unspecified states:** don't-care minimization does not establishpower-up initialization, safeinvalidstate recovery, or electricalfloatinginputbehavior. State/register examples give explicitinitialstates; anopeninput modeledHIGH is only valid when thequestionstipulates it.
10. **Source formatting:** extraction often dropsoverbars, subscripts, codepunctuation and amplitudeunits. Formalnoteequations are reconstructed and verified; scanned handwriting is not presented as anofficial verified answer.
