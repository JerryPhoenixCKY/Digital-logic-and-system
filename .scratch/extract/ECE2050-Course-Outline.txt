3. Learning Outcomes
Upon completing this course, the students will be able to:
• Understand the fundamentals of Boolean algebra and digital circuits theory (K);
• Analyze and apply common logic circuits (K/S);
• Design basic components in digital systems (K/S);
• Be aware of routine applications of digital systems (K/S);
• Self-study new digital components with the help from manuals (S);
• Appreciate the general considerations in digital system design (V).
